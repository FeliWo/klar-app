Klar Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026 · applies to Klar from the Mac App Store and to this website

In short

  • Klar has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your photos never leave your Mac.
  • Only if you turn on “Place, map, and weather” does Klar ask Apple and Mapterhorn. For that, the photo’s coordinates go out, plus the capture date for the weather. Klar asks you first, and you can turn it off anytime.
  • I receive no data from you unless you write to me.

2. What stays on your Mac

Klar shows your photos and reads their details (such as capture time, camera and location) to do so. This happens on your Mac. Klar also stores there:

  • your settings,
  • recently opened folders (can be turned off under Advanced),
  • the folders you have granted access to,
  • loaded place names, maps, weather and places nearby for up to 7 days, so they are ready right away next time (can be turned off under Advanced), and terrain data.

If you work with Apple Photos, Klar reads your library only with your permission and only on your Mac. If originals are in iCloud, macOS downloads them from Apple through your iCloud account. Everything Klar has created can be removed under Advanced with “Time to say goodbye”.

3. Place, map, and weather

If a photo has GPS data and you have agreed, Klar adds the place name, a map, places nearby, the weather at capture time and the terrain to the info overlay. For this, Klar sends:

AppleFor place names, the map and places nearby: the photo’s coordinates. For the weather (WeatherKit): the coordinates rounded to about 1 km and the capture date. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies.
MapterhornFor the terrain: only the number of a map tile, an area of a few kilometers around the location, with no date. Mapterhorn is an open-source project (maintained by Oliver Wipfli) delivered through Cloudflare. According to its privacy policy, Mapterhorn keeps request data for up to 30 days, including IP address, the requested tile and device type.

No photos, file names, your name or any account are sent. Like every request on the internet, these requests do carry your IP address, and Klar identifies itself as “Klar” with its version and the macOS version. Apple and Cloudflare may process the requests in the United States. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. According to its own privacy policy, Apple relies on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses for such transfers.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You give it in the question Klar asks before the first time, or with the checkbox under Advanced › Place and Weather, where you can also withdraw it anytime. Without it, Klar shows the coordinates plus the sun position and viewing direction, which it calculates itself.

If you choose “Open in Maps” in All About This Photo, Klar opens the Apple Maps app with these coordinates. What happens then is up to Apple.

4. If you write to me

“Report a Bug” in the Help menu prepares an email in your mail app; you send it yourself. It contains what you write, your screenshots, the Klar version and the macOS version. My mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail). I use your message only to reply and to improve Klar, and delete it once it is no longer needed for that. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. App Store

Purchases, downloads and updates go through Apple. If you allow sharing analytics with app developers in System Settings, Apple gives me crash reports without names. Apple does not tell me who you are.

6. This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). According to GitHub, it logs your IP address for security purposes when you visit. The pages themselves set no cookies, load no fonts or scripts from other services and count no visitors. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a secure, reachable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Just send me an email. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example where you live.

8. Changes

If what Klar sends changes, I will update this page and show the new date at the top.
