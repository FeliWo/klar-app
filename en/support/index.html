Klar Support

Klar is a simple photo viewer for the Mac: open, click through, see everything razor-sharp. Here are answers to the most common questions.

Contact

The quickest way is Help › Report a Bug in Klar. You can also write to me directly: hello.felix.sideprojects+klar@gmail.com. I usually reply within a few days.

Why does Klar ask for a folder?

Apps from the App Store may only see what you show them. When you open a photo, Klar may show that one photo. To browse the folder, Klar asks once. The Pictures and Downloads folders always work without asking. You find your grants under Settings › Default App and Access.

Where do place, map, and weather come from?

From the photo’s GPS data, completed by Apple and Mapterhorn. Klar asks you before the first time. You can turn it on or off under Settings › Advanced › Place and Weather.

Klar with Apple Photos

Under Settings › Apple Photos you open albums right in Klar, and with ⌘O even from within Apple Photos. The Little Helper makes this work while Klar is closed; it is very light and can be turned off anytime.

Remove everything

Under Settings › Advanced, “Time to say goodbye” removes what Klar has created. Then move Klar from the Applications folder to the Trash. Your photos stay exactly as they are.

Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Mac with Apple silicon.
